Design system v2, drawn at true size

Every phone below is 393×852, an iPhone 15 Pro in points, and 1 CSS pixel = 1 iOS point. There is no scale factor in this file. That is the entire point: the previous mockups were drawn at 272pt and multiplied by 1.379, so no size landed on a whole point and no two gaps in the app were deliberately related. Read a number off this page and type it into the stylesheet.

Spacing is only 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 96. Radii are only 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / pill, nested as inner = outer − padding. Type is the seven roles and nothing else. Rows are 44 / 56 / 72. Chips are one shape: 28 tall, 10 side padding, radius 8. There is not one shadow in this file: elevation is surface lightness in dark and a hairline in light. Rules in design-system-v2.md, screens in redesign-spec.md.
