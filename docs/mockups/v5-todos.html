v5 · one tab changes

To-dos, as a scrollable feed

The Feed was a full-screen deck: one card filling the screen, swiped sideways to reach the next, an action rail stacked bottom-right the way Reels and Stories stack theirs. It looked like a place to consume, not a queue to clear. v5 turns it into a vertical scroll of bounded cards, the shape LinkedIn and Instagram use for their main feed: several items visible at once, scanned top to bottom, each with its actions on a bar beneath it.

What is kept. The palette in both modes, the brand marks, the seven type roles, and the rule that colour means tier and only tier. The detail sheet and its composer are unchanged: tapping a card opens the sheet, and Reply or Comment open the composer rather than firing an empty send. What changes. The card is bounded, the tier bloom becomes a quiet top wash, the vertical rail becomes a horizontal action bar under the content, and the tier grouping the swipe order used to encode is now spelled out as section labels. There is no page title over the list: the section labels and the chips already name everything, so the list simply begins.

The tab · dark and light

The queue, top to bottom recommendedNo page header: the list starts on the first section label. Grouped Urgent, By EOD, Can wait, Later, with a count over each run. Tier reads from the top wash and the chip, no left edge line. Only Urgent and By EOD earn a filled primary action; nothing that can wait gets the strongest affordance.

Warm Sand, same structureThe wash alpha drops in light mode so the tint never muddies the card. Marks keep their own colour in both modes: they are identity, not priority.

The flow is untouchedTapping a card, or its Reply button, opens the same detail sheet. Reply opened the composer here rather than sending: the button lights white only because its composer is open, which is the one meaning of the active state.

The card, anatomy

Header: the source mark, its name, the item's own context (repo, sender, or channel), and the tier chip. Body: the subject at heading weight, then a one or two line preview of the message. Meta: blocking, age, repo, quiet and monospaced. Action bar: the same actions the rail carried, now horizontal and evenly split, glyph plus label, the primary one at full weight. An urgent card may carry a compact "why" line above its meta; everything deeper stays in the sheet, which keeps the feed scannable.
